# stockx_v1
**TradeSim Pro** is a next-generation paper trading platform designed for traders, investors, and market enthusiasts to practice and test strategies in a realistic trading environment without risking real money. Built with a sleek modern interface inspired by professional trading terminals, the platform combines real-time market simulation, interactive candlestick charts, portfolio analytics, and advanced order management into a single powerful experience.

The platform supports multiple asset classes including stocks, cryptocurrencies, and Futures & Options (F&O). Users can track live-style market movements, monitor portfolio performance, manage holdings, and execute simulated buy/sell orders with detailed brokerage and tax calculations. Integrated candlestick charting powered by  provides a smooth and responsive charting experience similar to professional trading platforms.

TradeSim Pro also features real-time crypto price updates through, dynamic order books, market depth simulation, multiple timeframes, and comprehensive trade history tracking. The platform is designed to help beginners learn trading while also giving experienced users a sandbox to experiment with strategies, risk management, and portfolio optimization.

With its futuristic UI, responsive design, and scalable architecture, TradeSim Pro can be expanded into a full trading ecosystem by integrating broker APIs, cloud databases, AI-powered market analysis, authentication systems, leaderboards, social trading features, and mobile or desktop app deployment using technologies like [Electron]

Whether you are learning the basics of trading, practicing advanced strategies, or building your own fintech product, TradeSim Pro delivers a professional-grade simulated trading experience directly in the browser.
